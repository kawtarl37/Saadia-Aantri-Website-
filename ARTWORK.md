# Botanical artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool for this prototype. Original portrait photographs are preserved unchanged.

Final asset: `public/botanical-tree.png` (transparent PNG).

Final prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: botanical collage tree for Saadia Aantri's editorial communication website. Create a single exquisite vintage botanical apple tree, entire tree with visible branching brown trunk and fine spreading roots, six main branching arms, finely engraved leafy canopy with pockets of open space, gentle textured paper-cut edges and vintage botanical lithograph details. NO apples, NO text, NO labels, NO frame, NO surrounding scene. Transparent background. Dominant potting-soil brown #4A2E27 wood and muted brown/olive foliage with pale yellow #F5EFC6 highlights, restrained cerulean blue #A5BCD6 details. Sophisticated adult educational personal brand, artistic collage not cartoon, broad spreading balanced canopy, front-facing, centered, full silhouette with margin around roots and canopy. A later frontend will overlay clickable red apples.

Arabic ornamental lettering is rendered separately as real Arabic text with SVG flourishes. Labels and interactive controls are HTML. The tree artwork has a code-native SVG fallback; apples are SVG on mobile and when WebGL or motion is unavailable, with Three.js enhancement on suitable desktop devices.
